Glossary of Terms — hover over underlined terms anywhere on this page for an explanation
WBS—Work Breakdown Structure — hierarchical decomposition of the total project scope into manageable activities
Baseline Schedule—The approved original project schedule against which all progress and delays are measured
Float—The amount of time an activity can be delayed without delaying the overall project completion date
3-Week Lookahead—A short-term plan showing all activities planned for the next 3 weeks — used in weekly planning meetings
Critical Path—The longest sequence of dependent activities — any delay on this path directly delays project completion
Resource Conflict—Two or more activities require the same crew/resource at the same time — one must be rescheduled
Mobilization—The process of deploying crew, equipment and materials to a site before construction starts
SPI—Schedule Performance Index — ratio of work done vs. work planned; <1.0 means behind schedule
MCC—Motor Control Centre — electrical panel that controls motors and equipment via starters/drives
