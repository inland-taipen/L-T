Glossary of Terms — hover over underlined terms anywhere on this page for an explanation
NCR—Non-Conformance Report — formal document raised when work or material does not meet the approved specification
QC—Quality Control — inspection and testing activities to confirm that work meets required standards
ITP—Inspection & Test Plan — a checklist of required inspections with Hold / Witness / Review points
Hold Point—A mandatory inspection stop — work cannot proceed until QC inspector has verified and signed off
Witness Point—An inspection that QC must be present for, but work can continue if inspector is unavailable after notice
Re-qualification—Item must be re-inspected after rework — it failed the first inspection
Pass Rate—% of inspections that cleared on the first attempt without requiring rework
OOS—Out of Specification — measured value falls outside the allowed tolerance range
Weld Defect—An imperfection in a weld (porosity, crack, undercut) detected during visual or RT/UT inspection
RT / UT—Radiographic / Ultrasonic Testing — non-destructive inspection methods to detect internal weld defects
